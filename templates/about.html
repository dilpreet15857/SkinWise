A simple introduction to SkinWise

Last updated: 12 November 2026

What is SkinWise?

SkinWise is a skincare guidance website created to make everyday skincare information easier to understand. It brings together a skin-type questionnaire, routine-building guidance and a product discovery tool in one place.

The goal is to help visitors think about their skin type, the concerns they want to address and the products that fit their preferences and budget. The tools use the answers a visitor chooses; they do not examine the skin or provide a clinical assessment.

How the tools work

Know Your Skin: A short questionnaire uses the visitor's responses to estimate a skin type from four broad categories: normal, oily, dry or combination.

Find Your Routine: Visitors can select their skin type, concerns and sensitivity preferences to receive a simple morning and evening routine with general ingredient information.

Find Your Products: The product tool filters items in the SkinWise catalogue using the selected skin type, main concern, budget and preferred brands.

Our approach

We believe a useful routine can be simple. Visitors should read product labels, check ingredients, introduce new products gradually and choose what feels appropriate for their own skin. Product details and prices may change, so always confirm information with the seller.
