How SkinWise handles information

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Information you provide

When you use the SkinWise assessment and recommendation tools, you may choose answers about your skin type, concerns, sensitivity, budget and preferred product brands. The website uses these answers to generate the result you request.

How the website uses your information

The current application uses Flask session data to remember selected results while you navigate between related tools. The application code supplied for review does not include a user account system or a database for storing assessment submissions. Your browser may retain a session cookie so the website can remember your session. Hosting infrastructure may process standard technical request information needed to deliver and secure the website.

Cookies and advertising

SkinWise may use cookies or similar technologies necessary for website functionality. If advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure and personalise advertisements, subject to their settings and policies. Review Google's advertising and privacy information for details and available controls.

External websites

Product cards may link to third-party brand or retailer websites. Those websites have their own privacy practices and terms. Review their policies before sharing information or making a purchase.

Children and sensitive information

SkinWise is a general informational website and is not designed to collect medical records or sensitive health information. Do not submit private medical details through the tools or contact channel.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the website's features or services change. For privacy questions, use the Contact Us page. Keep the contact details published there current.

Important: This policy is a draft based on the application code provided. Update it to reflect the actual hosting, analytics, advertising, email and cookie services enabled on your deployment.
