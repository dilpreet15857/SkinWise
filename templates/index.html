A LITTLE SKINCARE KNOWLEDGE

Build your routine with understanding.

Good skincare does not need to be complicated. Learn what each step does and make choices that suit your skin.

01

Cleanse gently

A cleanser removes sweat, excess oil, sunscreen and everyday buildup. Choose a gentle formula that leaves your skin feeling comfortable rather than tight or stripped.

02

Choose treatments thoughtfully

Serums and targeted ingredients can address particular concerns, but more products do not automatically mean better results. Introduce one new active product at a time and follow its directions.

03

Moisturise consistently

Moisturisers help reduce water loss and support the skin barrier. Lightweight textures may suit some people, while richer creams can feel more comfortable on dry skin.

04

Protect your skin from the sun

Broad-spectrum sunscreen helps protect exposed skin from UVA and UVB rays. Follow the product label, apply enough, and reapply as directed, especially after swimming or sweating.

There is no one-size-fits-all routine.

Skin can respond differently to the same product. Patch test new products, avoid introducing several active ingredients at once, and stop using a product that causes persistent irritation. If you have a skin condition or ongoing symptoms, seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
