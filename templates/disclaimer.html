Important information about SkinWise recommendations

Last updated: 12 November 2026

General information only

SkinWise provides general educational information about skincare routines, skin types and cosmetic ingredients. The questionnaire and generated recommendations are informational estimates based on the answers entered by a visitor. They are not medical advice, a diagnosis, or a substitute for consultation with a dermatologist or other qualified healthcare professional.

Individual results vary

People can react differently to the same ingredient or product. Skin type and skin condition can also change over time. Read product labels, follow the manufacturer's directions, patch test new products where appropriate and introduce active ingredients gradually. Stop using a product if it causes a reaction.

Product information

Product names, descriptions, prices, availability and links are provided for discovery purposes and may become outdated. Always verify current details, ingredients, suitability and pricing with the brand or retailer before purchasing. SkinWise does not guarantee the effectiveness or suitability of any listed product.

Persistent concerns

For persistent irritation, severe acne, allergic reactions, painful symptoms or other medical concerns, seek advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

Third-party links

Links to external websites are provided for convenience. SkinWise does not control and is not responsible for the content, products, policies or practices of third-party websites.
